**PanelProof** exists because display specifications are unusually easy to publish and unusually hard to check.

Almost nothing on a monitor box is regulated. "1ms response time" has no legally defined measurement condition. "HDR" on its own certifies nothing whatsoever. "100,000:1 contrast" may describe a mode nobody would ever use. Refresh rates are quoted for resolutions the port cannot actually carry. None of this is fraud, exactly — it is what happens when an industry markets against numbers that no independent body is required to verify.

A few of those numbers *are* governed by real, published, testable standards. VESA's DisplayHDR and ClearMR programmes have compliance test specifications you can download and read. HDMI and DisplayPort bandwidth is arithmetic, not opinion. Panel structure determines a set of behaviours that no amount of firmware tuning changes.

The gap between those two categories — claims that mean something and claims that do not — is what this site is about.

## What we do

We read the documents. The VESA DisplayHDR Compliance Test Specification, the ClearMR CTS, panel-maker datasheets, the HDMI and DisplayPort feature tables, manufacturer specification pages in their original wording. Then we set them against what the marketing says and report the difference, with the specification revision and the date each figure was current.

Where a claim can only be settled by measurement, we cite the labs that measure — by name, with a link, identifying what equipment and methodology produced the number.

## What we do not do

**We do not operate a measurement lab.** We own no colorimeter-and-photodiode rig, no high-speed camera, no integrating sphere. Any measured value on this site came from somebody else's instruments and is attributed to them.

We say this plainly because the alternative is the standard practice in this category: sites that imply testing they never did, republish RTINGS or TFTCentral numbers without attribution, and photograph a monitor on a desk to suggest a review unit. If you want measurements, go to the people who take them — [RTINGS](https://www.rtings.com/), [TFTCentral](https://tftcentral.co.uk/), [Monitors Unboxed](https://www.youtube.com/@monitorsunboxed). They are excellent, and we link to them constantly.

What they mostly do not do — because it is not their job — is explain the standards underneath the numbers, or track what a certification badge legally obliges a manufacturer to deliver. That is the job we took.

**We do not publish rankings we cannot defend.** No "best monitor 2026" list assembled from spec sheets. If we recommend, we say what the recommendation rests on.

**We do not accept payment for coverage.** No sponsored posts, no paid placement, no review units in exchange for favourable treatment.

## What we cover

- **[HDR & Brightness](/hdr/)** — DisplayHDR tiers in cd/m², local dimming, and why one HDR badge is meaningful and another is close to decorative.
- **[Panel Technology](/panels/)** — QD-OLED, WOLED, tandem OLED, Mini-LED and IPS: structure, and what structure determines.
- **[Motion & Latency](/motion/)** — response time measurement, overdrive, ClearMR, VRR and the difference between fast and clear.

Three further sections open as their first articles publish: **Spec Decoder** (what each number on the box measures and who certifies it), **Ports & Bandwidth** (what a given cable and port combination can actually carry), and **Setup & Calibration** (getting the display you already paid for). A section with no articles in it is a thin page, so it stays invisible until it has something to say.

## Who writes it

Articles are produced by the PanelProof editorial desk. We use AI research tooling to draft and to search standards documents; every figure is then checked against the cited source and reviewed by a human editor before publication. Our [editorial and AI policy](/editorial-policy/) sets out exactly what the tooling is and is not permitted to do, and our [corrections policy](/corrections/) explains what happens when we get something wrong.

We do not invent bylines, we do not fabricate expert quotes, and we do not manufacture personal anecdotes about testing we did not perform.

## How the site is funded

Display advertising, and in future possibly affiliate links — which will be labelled as such wherever they appear. Advertisers and affiliate programmes have no input into what we cover or what we conclude, and nobody sees an article before it is published. Where a piece discusses a product we could earn a commission on, that is disclosed on the page.
