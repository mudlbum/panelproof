The fastest way to reach us is email: **[editor@panelproof.com](mailto:editor@panelproof.com)**.

Putting one of these in the subject line gets your message to the right queue:

| Subject line | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `CORRECTION` | A figure or claim on the site is wrong. Highest priority — see our [corrections policy](/corrections/). |
| `QUESTION` | Something in an article is unclear, or you want to know what a specification actually requires. |
| `PRESS` | Media enquiries, or permission to quote us. |
| `ADVERTISING` | Advertising and sponsorship enquiries. Note that we do not sell editorial coverage — see our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/). |
| `PRIVACY` | Data access, correction or deletion requests under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/). |

We read everything. We reply to most things within two business days, and to corrections faster than that.

## Corrections get priority

If you believe something here is wrong, please say so — and if you have the standards document, datasheet or measurement that shows it, include the link. That single link typically turns a week of back-and-forth into a same-day fix. We log and date every substantive correction publicly.

## What we cannot help with

**Diagnosing your specific unit.** We cannot tell you whether your monitor's uniformity is within tolerance, whether that dead pixel qualifies under your warranty, or whether you should exchange it. We have never seen your display, and panel behaviour varies unit to unit.

**"Which monitor should I buy?"** We will not answer this, not out of unhelpfulness but because a stranger who does not know your room, GPU, content or budget has no business making the decision for you. Our articles are written to let you make that decision yourself.

**Measurement requests.** We do not operate a measurement lab and cannot test a display for you. [RTINGS](https://www.rtings.com/), [TFTCentral](https://tftcentral.co.uk/) and [Monitors Unboxed](https://www.youtube.com/@monitorsunboxed) do this work properly.

**Warranty, RMA and retail disputes.** These are between you and the manufacturer or retailer. We have no standing and no influence.

## Manufacturers and PR

We are happy to hear from you, and we will correct anything we have got wrong about your product quickly and publicly. Two things worth knowing in advance: we do not run sponsored content of any kind, and we do not agree to pre-publication review. Technical documentation, panel specifications and clarification of measurement conditions are always welcome and frequently useful.

## Suggestions

If there is a specification you have never seen explained honestly, tell us. A large share of what we publish started as somebody's emailed complaint that a number made no sense.
