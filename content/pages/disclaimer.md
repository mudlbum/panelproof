## We are not a testing laboratory

PanelProof does not measure displays. We have no colorimeter rig, no high-speed camera, no photodiode array. Every measured value on this site was produced by somebody else's instruments and is attributed to them by name.

Nothing here should be read as "PanelProof tested this". We did not. Where you need measurement, go to a lab that measures — we link to them throughout.

## Specifications change without notice, inside the same model name

This is the single most important limitation on this site, and it is not a technicality.

Manufacturers routinely revise a product during its production life while keeping the model number: a different panel supplier, a different backlight, a different scaler, new firmware that changes overdrive behaviour. Two units with identical model numbers, bought a year apart, can measure differently. Regional SKUs of the "same" monitor frequently differ in panel, port configuration and certification.

So an analysis accurate on its publication date may not describe the unit you receive. Every article states the date its figures were current. Check the specification on the retailer listing for your region before you buy, and check the certification listing rather than the marketing badge.

## This is not purchasing advice

We explain how specifications work. We do not know your room, your lighting, your GPU, your content, your eyesight or your budget, and we are not in a position to tell you what to buy. Where we express a view, it is a view, and it is reasoning you should check rather than a recommendation you should follow.

No article here is a substitute for seeing a display in person, or for a retailer's return window.

## Certification badges describe a floor, not a ceiling

A DisplayHDR tier certifies that a display met a set of minimum thresholds under specified test conditions. It does not certify that the display is good, that it will look like anything in particular with your content, or that any given scene will reach the certified peak. Two displays carrying the same badge can differ enormously. We explain what each badge does and does not oblige — but a badge is a floor.

## Accuracy, and its limits

We work hard on accuracy and we source to primary documents. Errors still happen: standards get revised, manufacturers correct their own pages, links rot, and we make mistakes. The site is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Verify anything you intend to spend money on against the manufacturer's current specification page and the relevant certification database, both of which we link.

If you find an error, please [tell us](/contact/) — we log and date corrections publicly.
